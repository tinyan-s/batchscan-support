Privacy policy

Documents and edits are stored on this device. FolioCarry has no account, advertising or automatic document upload. Purchases and restores use the App Store. Your chosen cloud file provider and sharing destination may use the network. Device backups follow system settings. When you choose to print, the system sends the selected files to your chosen printer. Local diagnostics contain task totals, duration ranges and generic error categories. Nothing is sent automatically. You can review and share the summary yourself. Deleting the app can remove drafts. Save important exported files to a location you control. Redaction masks in drafts are editable and the original materials remain on the device. Only new exports replace the marked content. Previously exported files and original source files are unchanged. If you contact support, we receive the email address and message you choose to send, solely to handle your request. Please do not send sensitive documents or identity records. You can use the same address to request deletion of support correspondence.

隐私政策

材料和编辑记录保存在本机。快扫PDF 无需账户，无广告，不自动上传材料。购买和恢复通过 App Store 处理。您选择的云盘文件提供方或分享目标可能联网；设备备份遵循系统设置。主动打印时，系统会把所选文件发送至您选择的打印机。 本地诊断仅包含任务次数、耗时区间及通用错误类别，不自动发送。您可预览后自行分享摘要。 卸载可能删除草稿，请把重要导出文件另存到您控制的位置。 草稿中的遮盖标记可以修改，原材料仍保存在本机。仅新导出文件替换标记内容，以前导出的文件及原始来源文件不变。 如主动联系支持，我们会收到您发送的邮箱地址和消息，仅用于处理该请求；请勿发送合同、证件等敏感材料。需要删除支持邮件时可通过同一邮箱联系。
