BatchScan — Support / 使用帮助

Shengnan Xian · Updated / 更新：2026-10-19

Scan. Organize. Send.

1. Scan or import. 2. Arrange pages and tap Next. 3. Preview, export, then save, share or print. Select two pages to place them on one A4 sheet. Redact in the page editor; add a purpose watermark under More options. Open email attachments in BatchScan, or save to Files and import. After exporting, Send by email attaches the files. Print from an exported file or Home → Exported files. Connect your iPhone and an AirPrint printer to the same Wi-Fi. Free: export up to 3 pages per document, without a brand watermark. Pro is a one-time purchase for complete longer-document exports. No subscription. Use Settings → Restore Purchases with the same Apple account. Scanning and previews do not require Pro. Canceling a purchase keeps your draft. Local pricing is shown by the App Store.

扫描、整理、发送文件

1. 扫描或导入。 2. 整理页面，点“下一步”。 3. 预览后导出，再保存、分享或打印。 选中两页可排版到同一张 A4。单页编辑支持遮盖；“更多设置”可添加用途水印。 邮件附件可选择“在 BatchScan 中打开”，或先存到“文件”后导入。导出后点“发送邮件”会附上文件。 打印：导出后或在首页“已导出”中打开文件，点“打印”。手机与支持 AirPrint 的打印机需连接同一 Wi-Fi。 每份文档最多免费导出3页，无品牌水印。Pro 一次买断，完整导出长文档，不自动续费。在设置中使用同一 Apple 账户恢复购买。扫描和预览不需要 Pro，取消购买保留草稿。当地价格由 App Store 显示。
